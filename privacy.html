Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

textkitpro.com ("we", "the site") publishes free browser-based text tools. This page tells you what the site collects, and what it does not.

How the tools work

Every tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The site processes the text you type or paste on your own device. It never sends that text to a server, and it never stores it. When you close the tab, the text goes with it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Analytics

The site runs no analytics. There are no analytics beacons, no page-view pixels and no visitor tracking scripts of any kind.

The site also has no server-side application and no account system. Every page is a static file. You cannot sign up, sign in or leave a record, because there is nothing to sign in to.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact link in the footer.
