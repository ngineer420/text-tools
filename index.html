    About TextKit Pro

    TextKit Pro bundles ten everyday text utilities into a single page. For writing there is a word counter with keyword density, text statistics with a Flesch readability score, and a Lorem Ipsum generator for mockups. For editing there is find and replace with full regular-expression support, sort and dedupe lines, and a diff checker that highlights line-by-line changes. For developers there is a case converter covering nine formats, Markdown ↔ HTML and CSV ↔ JSON conversion in both directions, and a slugify tool that turns a title into a URL-safe slug.

    Everything runs client-side in your browser — nothing you type or paste is ever uploaded to a server, so there's no wait and nothing to worry about privacy-wise.
